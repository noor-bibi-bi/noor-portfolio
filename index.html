SmogSense – AI Air Quality Forecasting for Lahore

AI-powered air quality forecasting and morning school activity alert system built for the Smart City Hackathon. Combines historical OpenAQ sensor measurements with Open-Meteo forecasts using a dual XGBoost architecture to predict next-day PM2.5 levels and issue automated recess advisories via FastAPI.

Dual XGBoost model: binary recess advisory ("Proceed" vs "Move Indoors") & 7-day trend regression
Dedicated out-of-time winter validation window for Lahore's severe seasonal temperature inversions
FastAPI microservice with automated morning email alerting dispatch for schools
